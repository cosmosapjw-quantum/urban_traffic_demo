# Noncompensatory candidate decision, D11
For a fixed one-run, one-origin/destination service registry, let x_ir be the passenger mass of intent i assigned to eligible run r. x_ir>=0, sum_r x_ir<=d_i, sum_i x_ir<=c_r; all units person trips. Sum over r: C_bus<=sum_r c_r, hence U_bus>=max(0,D_bus-sum_r c_r). With 24*40=960 and 30000 intents, U_bus>=29040 (96.8% of bus intent). This necessary condition does not establish all feasible allocations or arbitrary multi-stop transit capacity. More generally for subset I, U_I>=max(0,sum_I d_i-sum_{r in N(I)} c_r); no max-flow sufficiency claim for parent/chronological constraints. Prior parent/profile bound 47884.03508925/120000=0.39903362574375 is a stronger model-specific audited bound, not additive to bus deficit.
For two pulses on an empty length L with identical speed v>0, unrestricted receiving and arrivals 0,e in (0,T=L/v), independent characteristic arrival t2=e+T; sealed waiting arrival t2=2T. Thus delta=T-e>0 even as h tends to zero. With L=100m, v=10m/s, e=3s, delta=7s. This rejects the arbitrary-arrival continuum interpretation, not every finite-volume or FIFO model.
