## S02 lagged conservation and conditional dynamics

Let P_n be placed residents, Q_n pending externally requested inflow, H_n housing capacity. External increment I_n>=0, eligible new capacity Delta H>=0, and placement x_n between zero and min(Q_n+I_n,H_n+Delta H-P_n). Then P_(n+1)=P_n+x_n, Q_(n+1)=Q_n+I_n-x_n, giving P_n+Q_n=P_0+sum I. A relocation m_ij conserves total placed residents because it subtracts and adds the same amount; reject infeasible source/destination before commit. Units persons and persons of capacity, physical construction length in metres. Existing Growth::advance enforces old city stock identity, strictly prior completed stamp, monotonically consumed stamp and construction budget. No interpretation of unplaced increments as travel demand.

One-way work fixture: job support J=300, trip intent N_n=min(P_n,J), at most one unit vehicle/work activity per scheduled resident, with explicit inactive placed P_n-N_n. Frozen counterfactual N_n=150 while growth law still responds; it does not silently discard residents. Endogenous arm N_n=P_n (within support), same external increments, initial conditions and choice seed. Birth times floor(600*i/N_n), integer seconds, strictly increasing for N<=300. All declared trips drained by horizon or fail scoped gate; never reset occupied traffic. Each fast epoch uses same D07 graph installation/incident/communication schedule and begins from empty roads; represents repeated controlled travel period, not permanent construction of a physical new city every 3600s. Slow boundary at 3600*(n+1), access stamp 3600*n+1800, obtained from realized completed owner ages (mass-weighted mean conditional on completion). Missing completion gives empty support, never prior as realized observation. No observation from next demand enters same epoch. Housing threshold 72s, 25 external residents per slow update, 50 added housing places at a single predeclared entry. This sharp threshold/seed is a designed discriminant motivated by prior D07 means, not fitted empirical behavior. A single-origin aggregate can mask inequity; no multi-OD location-choice claim.

For discrete births b_i and completion d_i on integer ticks, sum_n active(n) h = sum_i min(T,d_i)-b_i for births before T (unfinished d_i=inf); active includes source pending and partial sink. Drained equality gives total realized age. Feedback changes N, so compare both conditional per-trip mean and total vehicle-second cost; lower mean alone cannot establish total welfare improvement. Distinguish induced scheduled trips through placement from behavioral demand elasticity, not calibrated induced demand.

For a smooth local surrogate of coupled accessibility and development, y_{n+1}=(1-a)y_n-b z_n, z_{n+1}=c y_n, with a in (0,1), b,c>=0 (rescaled variables, negative congestion response), the matrix characteristic polynomial z²-(1-a)z+bc has Schur stability iff bc<1 (positive 1-a range). This is an auxiliary one-lag linear result, NOT the discontinuous threshold Growth law or the actual D07 network. Wolfram verified the polynomial; independent rational and numerical checks below.

Literature discovery: Wegener (1986) https://doi.org/10.1068/a180437 abstract distinguishes fast transport, medium occupation, strongly lagged construction. Wegener (2004) https://doi.org/10.1108/9781615832538-009 stresses empirical identification difficulty. DfT 2024 https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6707bf8730536cb927482f91/dft-review-land-use-transport-interaction-models.pdf §2.1.6–7 distinguishes two-way LUTI and one-way LUMIT and common time lags. These motivate the comparison, not empirical calibration or transfer of a theorem.
