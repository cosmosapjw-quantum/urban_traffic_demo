## R05. Derive an observer-preserving transport reduction

Within a causal window fix physical equivalence classes fine enough to determine the next eligibility, route, receiving capacity and service amount from group totals. An event moves a fraction f in [0,1] of one homogeneous group i to group j or a sink. Let M be the group-by-passive-mark matrix (vehicles, persons, cargo, birth moments may be separate columns). Each conditional map has M' = T M. Column i of T contains (1-f) on i and f on j; other columns are identity. Include boundary contributions explicitly. Induct on events: the group totals of the detailed and reduced models agree, hence both pick the same event, f and new state. At the window end compute any linear query w^T M_final as (T_1^T ... T_E^T w)^T M_start. One reverse event updates only the donor weight. At a sink replace the receiver weight by the event payoff. A finite basis of delayed observation kernels can be propagated separately and recombined per tag. This retains exact joint marked totals in real arithmetic with O(q(E+N)) work for q independent observation masks, E sparse transfers, N source rows; eager repeated service can visit O(EN) label rows. Full detailed ordered exports still cost at least their output length. This result provides an alternative consumer contract for selected observations.
