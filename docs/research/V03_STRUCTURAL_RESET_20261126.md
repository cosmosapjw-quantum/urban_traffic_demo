## R05. Derive an observer-preserving transport reduction

Within a causal window fix physical equivalence classes fine enough to determine the next eligibility, route, receiving capacity and service amount from group totals. An event moves a fraction f in [0,1] of one homogeneous group i to group j or a sink. Let M be the group-by-passive-mark matrix (vehicles, persons, cargo, birth moments may be separate columns). Each conditional map has M' = T M. Column i of T contains (1-f) on i and f on j; other columns are identity. Include boundary contributions explicitly. Induct on events: the group totals of the detailed and reduced models agree, hence both pick the same event, f and new state. At the window end compute any linear query w^T M_final as (T_1^T ... T_E^T w)^T M_start. One reverse event updates only the donor weight. At a sink replace the receiver weight by the event payoff. A finite basis of delayed observation kernels can be propagated separately and recombined per tag. This retains exact joint marked totals in real arithmetic with O(q(E+N)) work for q independent observation masks, E sparse transfers, N source rows; eager repeated service can visit O(EN) label rows. Full detailed ordered exports still cost at least their output length. This result provides an alternative consumer contract for selected observations.

## Experimental synthesis

### Why the frontier advanced so slowly

In the original startup-to-peak experiment the physics contract, byte-exact wire, live-work allowance and diagnostic attribution had all been used as a single signal of progress. Address-space, RSS and per-phase allocations have different units of accounting even when all are reported in bytes. Likewise, a first guard excess of 410 bytes describes the first interrupted allocation. It cannot price the remaining operations in that tick. Storage work repeatedly focused on this narrow symptom while proportional partial service and downstream recombination continued copying labels. The old byte-wise replay expectations are valuable independent controls. Measuring the city/network outputs exposed a correctable physical mechanism that those tests did not probe.

The 25,720 to 25,730 s state grew from 4,677,350,066 to 5,523,765,378 raw wire bytes in one tick (18.10%). The wider 7.5 GiB treatment used an identical physical payload and an explicitly different checkpoint identity. Strict recording on the C05 build gained one tick and then stopped on timed incoming direct combine. The release matrix's 8 GiB RSS and 7,200 s wall targets remain testable product constraints. The internal 6 GiB default is too conservative for this one identified step; widening the scalar threshold alone delivers 10 simulated seconds.
