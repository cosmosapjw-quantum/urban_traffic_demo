# D05 final attempt: reserved crossing and resolution discrimination

## S01–S02. Boundary state is not unused quota credit

Authority AKM SSOT D05 (second and last attempt); original V1 contract unchanged. The original 0492364 PRD/traffic/routing designs and 6524500 constitution specify causal bulk transport, not a microscopic state for every resident. Preserve sparse fluid masses and track only existing indivisible service tags.

For a logical carrier of mass m>0, footprint ell>0, crossing progress a in [0,m], define the spatial occupancy measure at a link interface as upstream m-a and downstream a. At a source the upstream part is off-road; at a sink the downstream part is a terminal interface reservoir, not a completed service receipt. Keep the six kinetic moments and aggregate birth for the whole carrier, and do not split its scheduled journey owner. Its occupancy contributions are ell(m-a) and ell a, while its owner mass remains m until whole completion. At substep beginning compute free storage from these weighted occupancies (all classes). For a ready crossing with available send C_s, receive C_r [vehicles] and receiver space quota S_r [m], increase a by min(m-a,C_s,C_r,S_r/ell); missing constraints at source/sink are omitted. Deduct only this increment from current quotas. Upon completion atomically relabel carrier to the next route-position with progress zero, or emit one terminal service event. Incoming reserved occupancy converts to whole occupancy, never adds a second time. No future capacity, no stored unused quota, no partial passenger arrival. Maintain deterministic old-entry/tag order, bus-priority policy explicit; new carriers at end of tick cannot advance again in same tick. If blocked mid-crossing, retained reservation does not disappear. Whole vehicle distance before any exit reservation is >=L; crossing adds nonnegative delay. Old strict-atomic and proportional policies retain their distinct wire/identity. Frozen drift distance uses previous verified Strang mean, not stochastic first-passage.

Conservation proof per increment: spatial mass delta upstream=-z, downstream=+z, total including interface sink/pending unchanged; owner and birth unchanged. At completion spatial and logical ledgers reconcile: mass m and birth B move once. Receiver occupancy at next tick <= old + ell sum actual increments <= old+S_r<=K; departures only reduce it. Donor send and receiver count/metre budgets are nonnegative after each accepted increment; fluid uses the residual quotas only. For arbitrary competing flows, these imply safety, not liveness or fairness. One partial state per scheduled tag, O(number of active scheduled tags), no genealogical duplication; numerical tolerance for binary64 support/conservation explicit. Reject nonfinite progress, duplicate carrier, orphan crossing, crossing before ready position, malformed migration. For strict spare F0>ell*m, fixed positive c,w, h*w<=L, no competitors and appropriate open gates, previous r*=min(c,w(F0-ell*m)/(L ell)) gives T<=m/r*+h. At exact slack F0=ell*m, recurrence a_{n+1}=a_n+(hw/L)(m-a_n) when capacity nonbinding: a_n=m[1-(1-hw/L)^n]; if 0<hw/L<1, it never finishes in finite exact arithmetic. This is not a global traffic no-go; leave genuine/closure-induced blockage explicit. If free space zero, progress remains unchanged. The implementation uses fractional progress normalized to carrier mass only for arithmetic, physical increments charged in vehicles/metres; each partial progress is a physical boundary reservation, not a transferable independent vehicle or old capacity token.
