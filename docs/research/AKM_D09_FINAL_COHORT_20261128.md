A cohort has travelled distance d in [0,L]; W has no progress. The existing frozen-rate reaction preserves M and B and computes integrated mean speed. Each substep d'=min(L,d+U integral(mean u dt)). With no ready boundary until d=L, no outlet before accumulated mean travel length. New inflow joins W, which occupies link storage at full class footprint. At substep end, if A empty, promote entire W to A with distance zero. No new inflow joins A in flight; thus no same-substep multi-hop or reset of an active batch. Ready demand M(A), take min(M, remaining donor send, destination receive-count, destination receive-metres/footprint). Proportional split only when truly quota constrained; if quota >= M transfer A exactly. Same positive split for all moments and birth B. These are cohort-mean dynamics, not exact first passage for heterogeneous speed, nor a LWR equivalence.
