## R3/C3 · Typed definitions, non-equivalence of origin birth and FIFO entry priority, exact source-capture inequality

**Physical domain.** One FIFO segment with any finite `K≥1` strictly positive source cohort masses `m_i>0`, chronological priority coordinates `tau_1≤...≤tau_K` (seconds since shared origin, not necessarily route-level birth). Total `M=Σ m_i>0` (persons, cars or an independently chosen additive commodity). Define `S_i=Σ_{h≤i}m_h`, `S_0=0`, `Q_mu(u)=tau_i` for `S_{i-1}<u≤S_i`. Under ideal strict FIFO and independent service quota `0≤q≤M`, the chronological served priority-time moment is `A_mu(q)=Σ_i tau_i max(0,min(m_i,q-S_{i-1}))`. Dimension: `A` has commodity×second, `dA/dq` seconds. For route label `d_i∈{1,...,J}` attached to each cohort, the cumulative served destination mass is `F_j(q)=Σ_{i:d_i=j} max(0,min(m_i,q-S_{i-1}))` (commodity). These are *conditional instantaneous FIFO source primitives*, not claims that MetroFlow's entire heterogeneous junction is a single FIFO line with one globally fixed quota.

**Candidate theorem status:** (T1) `A_mu(q)=∫_0^q Q_mu(u)du` by exact source-row partition; `A` is continuous, piecewise linear and convex when the priority attribute tau is nondecreasing, with slope `tau_i` on each open source interval, and `A` determines the chronological priority-time measure μ up to merging indistinguishable equal-tau atoms. (T2) for equal total mass M, finite first absolute priority-time moment and arbitrary two distributions, `sup_{q∈[0,M]}|A_mu(q)-A_nu(q)|≤∫_0^M|Q_mu(u)-Q_nu(u)|du`, a sharp constant-1 control by unnormalized 1-Wasserstein distance. (T3) if the `F_j(q)` are available for all q as well, their a.e. slopes are the ordered destination indicator `1_{d(Q(u))=j}`, determining the joint chronological priority and next-cell label measure, up to co-located equal-tau cohorts and zero-mass distinctions. Interleaved same tau label sequences can still be observable from F_j(q) if priority within tie is fixed; otherwise different within-tie order is a distinct input not encoded by μ alone.

**Adversarial counterexample to overbroad theory:** Actual MetroFlow `Part.birth` represents an inherited origin birth *mass-moment*, not necessarily the current FIFO *entry clock*. If two prioritized cohorts enter the link at tau=(0,1) but carry origin-birth means (2,0), the cumulative origin-birth response has slopes 2 then 0, is **concave**, not convex. Accordingly, the theorem's convexity is about ordered FIFO priority timestamps tau only (or origin birth means additionally assumed monotone), never arbitrary multi-route native origin-birth attributes. A raw route/birth 1st moment is not itself a complete history.

## R5/C5 · New physical-theory theorem (constructed proof) and integrated default exact capture admission

**Theorem Q (chronological FIFO cumulative observability, convexity, W1 stability).** Fix a single physical FIFO queue with finite strictly positive cohorts `μ=Σ_i m_i δ_(tau_i)`, `tau_1≤...≤tau_K`, `M=Σm_i>0`, and a quota `q∈[0,M]` served from the first queue cohort forward with no overtaking. Let `Q_μ(s)` be the generalized increasing quantile (priority timestamp at cumulative mass coordinate `0<s≤M`). Define the complete all-quota FIFO priority-time response `A_μ(q)=Σ_i tau_i min(m_i,max(0,q-S_(i-1)))` with `S_i=Σ_{h≤i}m_h`. Then:
1. **Exact forward identity:** `A_μ(q)=∫_0^q Q_μ(s)ds` with dimensional consistency commodity×seconds and chronological quota boundary source `S_i`.
2. **Convexity and reconstructability:** Because `Q_μ` is nondecreasing, A is continuous and convex; for almost every q, `A'_μ(q)=Q_μ(q)`. Knowing A for *every* quota `q∈[0,M]` recovers the priority-time measure μ uniquely, modulo splitting of same-timestamp indistinguishable atoms (and omitted zero masses), by pushing Lebesgue measure `ds` on [0,M] forward through the almost-everywhere recovered Q. For finite cohorts, the piecewise linear slopes are precisely the sorted chronological priority times, and each slope interval width is the aggregate mass at that priority timestamp.
3. **Route-resolved completeness under explicit label:** If each unit of cumulative mass has an actual next-cell destination label `d(s)∈{1,...,J}` and we know all cumulative exit-by-destination curves `F_j(q)=∫_0^q 1_{d(s)=j} ds`, their a.e. derivatives supply the labels along the same mass-priority quantile coordinate. `A,F_1,...,F_J` determine the joint ordered priority time/next-cell stream up to null sets and splitting physically indistinguishable adjacent units. This does **not** recover full hidden route itinerary beyond the next-cell label or the order within an undefined priority tie; that ordering must be specified to define FIFO.
4. **Sharp physical stability:** For any two equal-mass chronological FIFO source distributions with finite first moment, `sup_{0≤q≤M}|A_μ(q)-A_ν(q)|≤∫_0^M |Q_μ(s)-Q_ν(s)|ds = W1(μ,ν)` (unnormalized one-dimensional mass transport distance). The inequality follows directly by triangle inequality for the prefix quantile integral; the well-known 1D mass-quantile formula gives W1. The constant is sharp: `μ=M δ_a`, `ν=M δ_b` gives equality `M|a-b|` at q=M. This is a *quantitative physical-model upgrade beyond last loop's fixed finite-moment non-closure*: the complete functional observable A is sufficient for the chronological priority distribution, while earlier R4 showed no fixed finite collection of global polynomial moments is sufficient. The transport formula is established general mathematics; **no claim of world-first novelty** in FIFO/optimal transport is asserted.

Original MetroFlow `Part.birth` is inherited origin-time mass moment and may violate monotonicity along current FIFO queue; its accumulated origin-birth response need not be convex. Unrestricted congested junction with competing routes is not identical to one fixed independent service quota. Origin path identity is not inferred from next-cell labels. ## R8/C8 · Second physical-theory result: finite quota-probe observability no-go, constructive and exact

**Theorem FS (finite-source quota sampling non-identifiability).** For **any fixed finite strictly increasing** list of service quotas `0=q_0<q_1<...<q_L=M` (L≥1, any rational/real spacings), there exist two different strictly positive finite-cohort chronological FIFO priority measures of the *same total commodity mass M*, with the *same next-cell destination label*, such that their **entire sampled vector** `{A(q_l), F_j(q_l): l=0,...,L, j=1,...,J}` is exactly identical, but their FIFO priority-time distribution and an unmeasured response `A(q*)` at some interior quota `q*` are different. Therefore no single fixed finite list of quota probes, even accompanied by all sampled route/destination resolved cumulative mass curves, suffices to reconstruct arbitrary priority histograms. This is a **different theorem** from the last loop's fixed finite *polynomial moment* non-closure and from R5's *all-quota functional completeness*: exact FIFO information can be encoded in a function, but not uniformly captured by an arbitrarily fixed number of function evaluations on unbounded cohort complexity.

**Constructive proof for arbitrary quota list.** Pick any positive gap `(a,b)=(q_k,q_{k+1})`, `L_*=b−a>0`. Keep both states identical for every other quota interval, with priority time 0 before the chosen interval and 4 after. On the gap, give one state a single positive cohort of mass L_* at priority timestamp 2; give the other two sequential positive cohorts of equal mass L_*/2 at timestamps 1 and 3. Both full priority sequences are monotone (0≤1≤2≤3≤4), have the same mass, and use the same next-cell label `a`. The gap-integrated first priority moment is `2L_*` in both cases, so `A(q_l)` is identical at every quota sample boundary and `F_a(q_l)=q_l` in both. But at unprobed `q*=a+L_*/4`, the first state adds `2L_*/4=L_*/2` while the second adds `1 L_*/4=L_*/4`, a strict `L_*/4` difference. Hence no fixed finite probe set is injective on this physical domain. QED.
