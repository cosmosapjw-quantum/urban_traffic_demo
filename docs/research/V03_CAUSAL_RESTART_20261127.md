## S05: new physical closure (independent of the exact V03 production solver)
Define U_n,A_n,D_n as requested, admitted and departed cumulative vehicle counts, initially zero. A homogeneous FIFO link has lag d>=1, storage K and integrated receiving/sending capacities I_n,C_n. With fixed exogenous request and no same-tick downstream release credit, set
D_{n+1}=min(A_{n+1-d},D_n+C_n),
A_{n+1}=min(U_{n+1},A_n+I_n,D_n+K).
A_j=0 for j<=0. Require nonnegative capacities, nondecreasing U and K fixed (or K>=current occupancy each tick). Induction: D_n<=A_{n-d}<=A_n<=U_n, hence D never decreases; A nondecreasing because each min operand >=A_n given K>=A_n-D_n. A_{n+1}-D_{n+1}<=K since D_{n+1}>=D_n. Pending U-A, inventory A-D, completed D partition U. For two closures with same U,d and ordered I,C,K at each tick, componentwise A,D ordering follows by monotonicity of min and induction. Therefore backlog U-D and its left-Riemann time integral are ordered; FIFO quantile exit time inf{n:D_n>=q} cannot increase under expanded capacities. This is a constructive positive physical proposition, not a no-go. It establishes exact count-level lumpability for this homogeneous delayed FIFO closure and O(d) storage, independent of route-row fragmentation. It is not an exact quotient of the finite-response multiclass V03 kernel with endogenous turns/marks, nor evidence of induced demand.
