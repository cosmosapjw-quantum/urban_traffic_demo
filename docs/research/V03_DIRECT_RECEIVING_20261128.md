## S02. Interval transport as a marked measure; constructive representation theorem
A receiving stream of marks k=(route,offset) has positive measures dM_k and dB_k on admission clock x (mass veh, birth first moment veh*s). Each positive interval [u,v] carries constant densities m/(v-u), B/(v-u); point arrivals stay Dirac atoms and retain endpoint bits. On the common refinement of all endpoints E, define M_{kj}=sum_i m_i*(b_j-a_j)/(v_i-u_i) and analogously B, only for i whose support contains the open cell (a_j,b_j). At any endpoint e in E, the cumulative marked inflow F_k(e) equals the sum of refined-cell masses before e plus the explicitly ordered atoms through e. This follows by finite additivity of lengths on each source interval. For any contiguous window with endpoints in E, the cumulative difference gives its exact marked mass and birth moment. Consequently the residence-age functional t*M-B is also preserved. This is a real-arithmetic conservation statement, not an assertion that merging different FIFO heads, or replacing original rounded multiplication/division with a single fraction, is bit-exact. For positive nonempty head, a common fractional service restriction acts on all its marks, so a representation that decodes every ordered (tag,offset,m,B,a,b) identically preserves all original per-row numerical operators; induction over event sequence preserves the physical trajectory under identical accepted inputs, provided resource admissions succeed.

## S09. Constructive local density-error control for logical record pressure
Dictionary compression alone does not reduce logical rows. At fixed class/route mark k, consider an interval/bin [a,b] of length h seconds with nonnegative bounded density r(x) (veh/s), range [r_min,r_max], and mean r_bar=M/h. Replace density within this one bin by its mean, preserving mass M and (separately) birth-moment integral B; keep point atoms, protected readiness/route/class/owner cuts distinct. The cumulative prefix error E(x)=integral_a^x(r-r_bar) has E(a)=E(b)=0. For ell=x-a, E(x)<=min((r_max-r_bar)ell,(r_bar-r_min)(h-ell)); for -E reverse the two slopes. If A=r_max-r_bar, C=r_bar-r_min, A+C>0, max_ell min(A ell,C(h-ell))=h A C/(A+C)<=h(A+C)/4. If range zero E=0. Also |E|<=M by positivity. Thus ||E||_infty<=min(M,h*(r_max-r_min)/4), in vehicles. The constant 1/4 is sharp for equal half-bin widths at the two extremes. For independent marks, sum per-mark certified bounds controls the L1 prefix discrepancy. Disjoint bins conserve every marked mass and birth-moment integral; at protected boundaries the cumulative discrepancy vanishes. With birth density (veh*s/s), the same bound applies in veh*s. Age at an endpoint remains t*M-B.
An adaptive algorithm accepts a bin only if both marked prefix budgets fit and no protected causal boundary is crossed; otherwise split. Constant density bins need no error allowance; no claim that adjacent physical FIFO heads can be merged with identical rounded service dynamics. The result is a constructive local approximation bound, not an exact whole-network error estimate: node capacity minima, order changes, signal thresholds, route choices and supply feedback need separate stability/observability analysis.
