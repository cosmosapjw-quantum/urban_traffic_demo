## S01–S02. Construction and hypotheses

The research authority is AKM SSOT v1.0.0, D05 only. The original PRD/spec (0492364), constitution (6524500) and active mesoscopic design (3d83db0) prescribe finite storage, causality, meaningful cost, not exhaustive vehicle tracking. Prior D04 and D06 are verified within scope. Keep the original acceptance plan untouched.

A baseline computes nonnegative post-donor-cap proposals d_i, grouped by donor e, with source proposals s_r. A movement enters receiver j with footprint l_i. Let first-pass common donor factors f_e and source receiver factors g_j satisfy all nonnegative count/space constraints. Define residual count C'_j=C_j-sum incoming y and residual metre supply S'_j=S_j-sum l_i y. Remaining proposals are d_i(1-f_e) and s_r(1-g_j); a donor with a positive remaining movement to a zero residual count or metre receiver cannot make any common-factor increase and can be excluded. For each receiver, sum remaining eligible count/metre demands D'_j,H'_j and choose r_j=min(1,C'_j/D'_j,S'_j/H'_j) with zero denominators omitted. Every residual donor takes min over its demanded receivers; source uses r_j. The final factor is f_e+(1-f_e)f'_e and g_j+(1-g_j)r_j. Each increment is feasible by comparison with each residual receiver limit, and final send <= d. No iteration to convergence needed: one additional bounded pass, O(P+E+R), fixed arrays, no heap allocation in step. No claim of maximal throughput, global fairness or strict FIFO. If no receiver constraint is active, unchanged. A donor blocked by one turn remains blocked, but need not consume capacity reserved by a separate donor. Positive velocity-selected transfer uses D04 quadrature with effective Courant c_eff=q/J (J>0); same mass flux, outgoing first velocity moment c_eff Z. Birth independence assumption unchanged. Distinct model identity, no silently changed default.

For D optional service hybrid, finite mean characteristic instead of geometric mass leakage: integrate Strang-frozen PDMP drift. Half switching gives p_mid; u0=J/M. Under affine drift over h, u(s)=u0 exp(-s/tau)+q p_mid(1-exp(-s/tau)); integral D=U[u0 A+q p_mid(h-A)], A=tau(1-exp(-h/tau)). Thus 0<=D<=Uh for admissible moments. Stable h-A=h*[1-expm1(-x)/(-x)] with small-x series x/2-x²/6+x³/24-...; no cancellation at small x. Use same half switch/affine drift/half switch update as previous react. The integral is exact for the drift suboperator, a positive splitting approximation to the true frozen PDMP mean distance (not exact stochastic first passage). Since per-step movement cannot exceed Uh, a carrier initialized at x=0 cannot exit a link L before t>=L/U. A bus unit moves as a whole only when cumulative characteristic distance reaches L and old-state count and metre quotas allow; otherwise it waits. Do not advance more than one link per substep. Bus FIFO within link by entry time/tag and indivisible source admission. Reserve bus quota before fluid offers; explicit priority policy, no all-class FIFO. Unit transfer can starve when per-step quotas never reach one; deliberately preserve this negative limit, not change capacity or add fictitious credit. All other road classes remain A proportional mixing to isolate service effect. Use new checkpoint identity. Migration refuses partial active buses because earlier mixed rank/distance not recoverable.
