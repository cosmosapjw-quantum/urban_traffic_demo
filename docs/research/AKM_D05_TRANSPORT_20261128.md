A baseline computes nonnegative post-donor-cap proposals d_i, grouped by donor e, with source proposals s_r. A movement enters receiver j with footprint l_i. Define residual count C'_j=C_j-sum incoming y and residual metre supply S'_j=S_j-sum l_i y. Remaining proposals are d_i(1-f_e) and s_r(1-g_j); a donor with a positive remaining movement to a zero residual count or metre receiver cannot make any common-factor increase and can be excluded. For each receiver, sum remaining eligible count/metre demands D'_j,H'_j and choose r_j=min(1,C'_j/D'_j,S'_j/H'_j) with zero denominators omitted. Every residual donor takes min over its demanded receivers; source uses r_j. The final factor is f_e+(1-f_e)f'_e and g_j+(1-g_j)r_j. Each increment is feasible by comparison with each residual receiver limit, and final send <= d.
