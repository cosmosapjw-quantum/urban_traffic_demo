## R5/C5 · Constructive priority-junction theorem proved + production lifetime correction TDD GREEN

**Theorem WPF (weighted progressive demand/supply node, constructive):** Let `I` finitely many upstream links and `J` receiving resources. Assume `w_i>0,S_i≥0,R_j≥0,β_ij≥0`, `Σ_j β_ij=1` after including real sink destinations where relevant; hold these coefficients fixed during ONE original event substep. Define `P={q:0≤q_i≤S_i, Σ_i β_ij q_i≤R_j}`. Starting q=0, keep unsaturated active set A. Increase each active `q_i` at `w_i` per common parameter λ until the minimum positive available demand or receiving-capacity bound:
`Δλ=min_{i∈A} (S_i−q_i)/w_i, min_{j: σ_j>0}(R_j−Σ_i β_ij q_i)/σ_j`, `σ_j=Σ_{i∈A}β_ij w_i`.
Then remove each source i at its demand saturation or if it uses a saturated receiver j with β_ij>0; repeat. Under exact rational/real arithmetic each round maintains P feasibility: for each j the new used capacity is old used plus `Δλ σ_j≤R_j−old_used`, and `q_i+Δλ w_i≤S_i`. At least one demand or receiver constraint is attained each round, and any newly tight receiving constraint freezes all its active positive users; hence A strictly decreases. The method terminates in at most `|I_+|` active rounds (finite), no iteration horizon truncation, and preserves nonnegativity and mass conversion `Σ_j Σ_i β_ij q_i=Σ_i q_i`.

**Weighted max–min optimality proof:** For an output `q^★`, consider any coordinate i someone proposes to increase. If it is demand saturated q_i^★=S_i, infeasible. Every other source h using j either already froze earlier at `q_h/w_h≤q_i/w_i`, or froze simultaneously at equal normalized rate. A feasible increase in q_i requires strictly decreasing q_h for at least one such h with `q_h/w_h≤q_i/w_i`. Therefore q^★ satisfies weighted max-min fairness. The progressive algorithm is deterministic in exact arithmetic, and weighted max-min flow is the lexicographic maximizer of sorted normalized rates q_i/w_i under the convex, nonnegative monotone constraints; it is unique as a vector because lexicographic equal sorted rates are fixed by the progressive freeze ordering and the tight constraints.
