## S05: marked cumulative-rank network closure
Primary source checked: Yperman, Logghe, Tampere, Immers, manuscript submitted 2005-11-18, The Multi-Commodity Link Transmission Model for Dynamic Network Loading, pp.5-6 eqs.(1)-(5), pp.10-13 diverge rule and spillback case, https://www.mech.kuleuven.be/cib/verkeer/dwn/pub/P2006A.pdf . In this model route-disaggregated cumulative counts sum to total and FIFO ranks give passage time. Wen-Long Jin, Unifiable multi-commodity kinematic wave model, TRB 117B (2018) 639-659, doi:10.1016/j.trb.2017.08.013, distinguishes shared-speed FIFO from aggregate speed-density unifiability. Neither source establishes equivalence to MetroFlow finite-response kinetic model. Our contribution here is a project-specific marked closure with a retained birth moment, old-clock capacity, and finite queue-fragment bound, not a global novelty claim.
For one homogeneous FIFO rank interval of mass M>0, define constant per-route mark density dm_r/dq=m_r/M and db_r/dq=b_r/M (mass in veh, birth moment in veh*s), with sum_r m_r=M. All marks share the same link readiness clock. A prefix of fraction alpha carries exactly (alpha m_r,alpha b_r); remainder carries (1-alpha)(m_r,b_r). Composition at adjacent rank intervals is additive, so replacing any subdivision with identical densities and readiness by one interval is a sufficient exact real-arithmetic representation of this closure. Distinct density or readiness cannot be coalesced by this lemma. No arbitrary V03 row merging or binary64 bitwise equivalence is implied.
At a diverge, each fixed route r has one next link j(r); let m_j=sum_{r:j(r)=j} m_r, f_j=sum_{r:j(r)=j} ell_r*m_r, ell_r in lane-m/veh. With remaining sending mass S, receiving R_j (veh) and old-clock remaining storage F_j (lane-m), feasible common fraction alpha has 0<=alpha<=1, alpha M<=S, alpha m_j<=R_j, alpha f_j<=F_j. Thus alpha*=min(1,S/M,min_{j:m_j>0} R_j/m_j,min_{j:f_j>0} F_j/f_j). Each inequality bounds alpha independently; their minimum is itself feasible and maximal. Serve a prefix of the eligible head, stop if a partial head remains; proceed to next head only after full retirement. This gives unique head-local throughput, not general optimal network assignment. In particular one constrained turn restricts other marks in the same head even if their own capacity is unchanged. For head (1,1), S>=2, R=(0.25,1), no storage bottleneck, departures=(0.25,0.25); raising only R_0 to 1 increases both to (1,1). This mechanism is already known qualitatively in FIFO LTM; the marked construction retains original route and moment.
For each link, freeze F=K-sum_r ell_r X_r at old clock, decrement by transfers from any upstream origin, and do not credit exits until next tick. Sending is restricted by readiness d>=1 ticks, remaining capacity and actual FIFO head. Because total admitted weighted mass <=K-old stock and outgoing weighted mass>=0, each new stock <=K; nonnegative mass follows from alpha in [0,1]. Every interior transfer cancels identically between upstream and downstream in both mass and birth-moment ledgers; for zero initial traffic U_r=D_r+P_r+sum_l X_{lr}, B^U_r=B^D_r+B^P_r+sum_l B^X_{lr}. Requests and exits at right tick endpoint. Each mass m born at t_b and exited at t_e contributes m(t_e-t_b); unfinished contributes m(T-t_b). Summing gives exactly W_r(T)=dt sum_{n<N}(U_r(n)-D_r(n))=H^D_r(T)+T*(P_r+sum_l X_lr)-B^P_r-sum_l B^X_lr. This is independent of internal route transfers and a nontrivial check for a conservative implementation.
For a two-route diverge with one mixed birth batch per tick, a FIFO service loop retires any number of full heads and at most one partial head each tick (the latter exhausts a constraint and stops). Across N ticks source receives <=N batches; source->trunk produces <=2N fragments, trunk->each branch <=3N fragments. Therefore queue caps N+1,2N+1,3N+1,3N+1 suffice without heap growth during the physical update. This bound is on one projected topology and explicit horizon, not arbitrary network cycles/merges. Finite response, class speed heterogeneity, route-choice feedback, reroute/merge and continuous entry-time distributions remain unresolved.
