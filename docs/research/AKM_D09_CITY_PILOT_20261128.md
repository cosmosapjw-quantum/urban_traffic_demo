Let a calendar leg i request amount a_i in a unit (person trip, veh equivalent, or kg), cumulative profile F_i(T) in [0,1], and structurally admitted fraction s_i in [0,1]. Private/intrazonal with legal owner s_i=1; absent owner s_i=0; fixed bus/freight s_i is assigned capacity-independent fraction (actual service can do less). With parent p, eligibility CDF <= completed parent fraction. Define U_i(T)=min(F_i(T),s_i,U_p(T)) recursively (omit parent term at root). Then completed leg fraction <= U_i(T), and minimum uncompleted demand >= sum a_i(F_i(T)-U_i(T)). This is an optimistic support ceiling, not a forecast; seat competition, dwell, travel, discrete service timing and road congestion only tighten it. Positive bound rules out full completion under the same fixed declarations; it does not rule out calendar retention or different supported transport. Independent rational cases verify inequality. Unsupported counts alone do not quantify physical mass.
