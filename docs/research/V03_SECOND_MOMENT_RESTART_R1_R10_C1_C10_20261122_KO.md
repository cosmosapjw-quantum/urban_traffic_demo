## R3/C3 · second-moment transient physics and decoder source lifetime

For a fixed route label r with nonnegative cohort mass dm born at clock b, define \(\displaystyle N_r(t)=\int dm,\ H_r(t)=\int b\,dm,\ J_r(t)=\int b^2\,dm\), ages \(\displaystyle A_r(t)=tN_r(t)-H_r(t)\) and age-square \(\displaystyle Q_r(t)=t^2N_r(t)-2tH_r(t)+J_r(t)\). Units respectively mass, mass·s, mass·s², mass·s, mass·s². Genuine age requires each active b≤t. When N_r>0 the route age variance is \(\sigma_r^2=Q_r/N_r-(A_r/N_r)^2\ge0\) by the realizability inequality \(\displaystyle N_rQ_r-A_r^2=N_rJ_r-H_r^2\ge0\), Cauchy–Schwarz for nonnegative physical cohort mass. Empty N_r=0 makes mean and variance undefined, not zero.

For external boundary mass measures dI_r,dO_r with origin first birth moments dB^I,dB^O and second birth moments dC^I,dC^O, conservation across all *internal route-preserving transfers* gives dN=dI−dO, dH=dB^I−dB^O, dJ=dC^I−dC^O. Stieltjes differentiation yields \(\displaystyle dQ=2A\,dt+(t^2dI-2t\,dB^I+dC^I)-(t^2dO-2t\,dB^O+dC^O)\). On finite horizon [t0,T], with finite second birth moments and finite-variation boundary event measures,
\[
\boxed{D^O_{r,2}[t_0,T]=2\int_{t_0}^T A_r(t)\,dt+D^I_{r,2}[t_0,T]+Q_r(t_0)-Q_r(T),}
\]
where \(\displaystyle D^{I/O}_{r,2}=\int(t^2dI/O-2t\,dB^{I/O}+dC^{I/O})\) is squared age carried by entry/exit. **Proof:** differentiate t²N−2tH+J with product rule, apply route-wise conservation; each internal transfer has matching positive/negative mass, birth-1 and birth-2 contributions; integrate and rearrange. No stationarity or FIFO assumed for the balance itself; FIFO source order enables computing route output birth moments. This is a substantive second-moment physical closure, not an allocation theorem.

For completed route output mass M^O>0, physical mean residence \(\mu=D^O_{r,1}/M^O\) and variance \(V=D^O_{r,2}/M^O-\mu^2\ge0\). The empirical mass fraction with residence ≥u for u>0 is at most \(D^O_{r,2}/(M^O u^2)\), from nonnegative squared residence. This is a bound, not an exact percentile. Without independently supplied J, native historic N,H cannot determine the variance: original Rust checkpoint carries first origin birth moment only. **Do not claim completed full-city second-moment prediction from this oracle.**
