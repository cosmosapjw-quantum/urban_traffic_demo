## R3/C3 · Theorem 1: conditional non-closure and sharp route flux rank bound [DERIVED]

**Assumptions/variables.** Consider the same upstream traffic cell `i` whose current original packet consists of `K≥J≥2` nonnegative route masses `m_k` with fixed total `M=Σ_k m_k>0`, same road class/vehicle footprint, same fixed source readiness (no signal/closure crossover), and `J` distinct next-cell destinations `d(k)`. Assume an admissible *short-time uncongested* original server with a positive common served fraction `0<α≤1` **independent of route composition** at fixed M (all relevant downstream supplies and capacities unbinding). The native source uses `V1Road::dest`, `beta_j=(Σ_{k:d(k)=j}m_k)/M`, `served_parts(parts,α)`. Let `D∈{0,1}^{J×K}` have `D_{jk}=1_{d(k)=j}`, with each of J destinations realized by at least one route. Its immediately outgoing destination-mass vector is
`F(m)=α D m`.
This is a submodel of the route-fraction computation, not an assertion that all congested/signal-controlled states have the same α.

**Theorem 1.** On the fixed-total hyperplane `H_M={m>0:1^T m=M}`, the destination-resolved one-step transfer cannot be determined solely from total mass M and arbitrary fewer than `J−1` independent **linear** route-composition statistics. More precisely, if a linear observable `A:H_0→R^r` and arbitrary map `g` satisfy `F(m)=g(A(m−m0))` for all interior `m∈H_M`, then `rank(A|_{H_0})≥J−1`, where `H_0={v:1^T v=0}`. Equivalently, for unfixed M a linear sufficient state `A_total` that explicitly includes M must have rank ≥J.

**Proof.** For any `v∈ker(A)∩H_0` and any interior positive `m0`, sufficiently small `ε>0` keeps `m±=m0±εv∈H_M`, with `A(m+)=A(m−)`. Sufficiency forces `F(m+)=F(m−)` and hence `D v=0` because α>0. Thus `ker(A|_{H_0})⊆ker(D|_{H_0})`. By rank-nullity `rank A|_{H_0}≥rank D|_{H_0}`. Since all J distinct destination groups have representatives and `1_J^T D=1_K^T`, the image `D(H_0)= {z∈R^J:1_J^T z=0}`, rank `J−1`. This proves the stated lower bound. The bound is **sharp**: retaining J−1 independent destination masses plus M determines the Jth and all J outgoing fluxes. No unproved statement about nonlinear/discontinuous encodings is made.

**Concrete counterexample.** For J=2, masses `m=(1,3)`, `m'=(3,1)`, equal M=4, same class and total spatial occupancy. If `d(0)=a`, `d(1)=b`, the original uncongested traffic transfer to a/b differs by `α(−2,2)`; thus a density-only scalar model cannot be exact for both states. Under uncongested α=1/2, the outgoing masses are `(0.5,1.5)` vs `(1.5,0.5)` at the same incoming total. These are **exact rational quantities**, not a statement about measured Valley delay.

**Literature/novelty gate:** The rank bound is a newly derived, explicit source-specific statement with exact hypotheses and a sharp lower bound, but general multi-commodity distribution is established in the traffic literature. It is not claimed as a first discovery. ## R4/C4 · Theorem 2: finite birth-time moments are not an exact FIFO dynamical closure [PROVED IN IDEAL FIFO DOMAIN]

**Domain.** One ideal no-overtaking link/source queue with an arbitrary finite number of chronological, distinguishable positive-mass cohorts whose FIFO priority follows their known increasing cell-entry times `t_i`. A nonzero service capacity `q` consumes mass from the oldest cohort to the newest, allowing a fractional final cohort. Write `μ=Σ_i m_i δ_{t_i}`, `m_i>0`; its time moments are `M_r(μ)=Σ_i m_i t_i^r` for `r=0,...,p`. The original MetroFlow `Part.birth` is an origin-time first moment per retained cohort, NOT an attempt to use only `M_1` globally. This theorem concerns what would happen if any finite set of global moments replaced the full ordered cohort list, not the current exact native.

**Theorem 2 (quantifier explicit):** For **every** fixed nonnegative integer `p`, there exist *two distinct positive finite cohort measures* `μ_+`, `μ_−` on the same `p+2` strictly increasing timestamps and a common positive FIFO service quota `q`, such that `M_r(μ_+)=M_r(μ_−)` for all `r=0,...,p`, but the served mass's `t`-first moment **differs**. Hence no map from any prescribed fixed finite polynomial time-moment vector `(M_0,...,M_p)` to the exact all-quota FIFO departure-age distribution exists on this domain.

**Constructive proof.** Fix `Δ>0`, timestamps `t_i=iΔ` (`i=0,...,p+1`), and let `w_i=(-1)^i binom(p+1,i)`. The generating polynomial `(1-z)^{p+1}=Σ_i w_i z^i` has a zero of multiplicity `p+1` at `z=1`. Applying `(z d/dz)^r` and evaluating at `z=1` gives `Σ_i w_i i^r=0` for every `0≤r≤p` (with `i^0=1` also at i=0). Choose `ε>0` and strictly positive weights `m_i^{±}= [2±(-1)^i] ε binom(p+1,i)`. Their difference is `2ε w_i`, so all `M_r` agree exactly up to degree p, with `M_0(μ±)=2^{p+2}ε`. At oldest time `t_0=0`, `m_0^+=3ε`, `m_0^-=ε`. Serve `q=2ε` in FIFO order. Under `μ_+`, all q comes from t0, so the first moment of served traffic is **0**. Under `μ_−`, exactly ε exits from t0 and ε from t1=Δ, so its first moment is **εΔ>0**. Same total service mass q, same all first p input moments, different physically observable served-age moment. Contradiction to any proposed exact moment-only closure. QED.

**Smallest test for p=1:** three chronological cohorts at t=(0,1,2) with positive masses `μ+=(3,2,3)`, `μ−=(1,6,1)`. Both have M0=8 and M1=8, and serving q=2 gives source birth moment 0 versus 1 (one unit exits at t1 in μ−). Simpler independently checkable alternative `(4,1,4)` vs `(2,5,2)` has M0=M1=9 but q=3 yields 0 vs 1. Even including total density and first-birth moment cannot restore the original FIFO release-age process.

**Independent exact symbolic check:** Wolfram symbolic `Σ (-1)^j binom(p+1,j) j^r=0` was verified for `p=0,...,8`, all `0≤r≤p`, with the combinatorial convention `0^0=1` explicitly handled. Wolfram also confirmed the q=3 p=1 alternative's equal M0=9, equal M1=9, and distinct served temporal moments 0 and 1. This bounded CAS check supports but does not replace the general polynomial-zero proof.

**Interpretation/status:** This is a genuinely **physical theory/closure theorem** about the dynamical information needed by an exact FIFO model, independent of how Rust buffers are compacted. It rules out finite polynomial *temporal moment-only* replacement of all cohort history in arbitrary-length queues; it does **not** prove that every finite-dimensional nonlinear/discontinuous state, other hidden state, or all network-restricted histories are impossible, nor any absolute memory growth theorem for V03.
