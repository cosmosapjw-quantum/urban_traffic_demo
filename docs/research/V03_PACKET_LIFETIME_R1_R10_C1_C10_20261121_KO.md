## R2/C2 · Original FIFO literature and source lifetime investigation

Verified from the exact parent Rust code at `crates/metroflow-rust/src/v1_road.rs`: `RoadWorkBudget::transfer` adds new incoming raw Part/Mark row counts to the single monotone `self.incoming` counter; `RoadWorkBudget::require` uses `segmented_incoming_charge_for(self.incoming,self.incoming_page_cells,...)` for **every later phase**. But during second part of `V1Road::step_state`, after the complete source transfer, the road processes *each destination cell in index order*. For each cell `i`, `marks[i]` are read to compute weighted speed and latent moments and then never accessed again. `incoming[i]` source pages are moved into local `source`, all original rows are losslessly combined into `parts` via sorted source indices and exact original IEEE `sum`, then `drop(source)` destroys those source pages. The source still holds the **unneeded original `marks[i]` fixed page arrays** until the end of the whole step, and the `work.incoming` charge is never reduced despite `incoming[i]` being physically freed. Under trace record, the diagnostic-only `incoming_src[i]` is also kept after its `exact_merge_census` use. This is an actual source-lifetime defect and live allocation opportunity, independent of exactly how many physical packet cohorts must survive.

The original 25,690→25,700 failing tick after a 30s continuous prefix: `road_work:packet interval packing` required 5,026,043,552 against 5,026,041,759, excess 1,793B, cumulative 13,915,377 incoming rows and 92,142 interval-packing checks, while the fresh identical-input reloaded tick stalled at `entry interval split` required 5,087,568,488 against 5,087,563,191, excess 5,297B. Different source session lifetimes mean these are distinct guard traces, not proof of changed physics. The physically distinct source rows in packets and their route/f64 birth histories must NOT be removed. An exact source buffer may be dropped **only after** all computations requiring its original values are complete; its presence must be removed from subsequent source-bound resource accounting **at that actual lifetime boundary**. The original correct FIFO work cannot be changed from a hash-key collision or merely a lower numeric guard.

Carey (2017), *Representation requirements for perfect first-in-first-out verification in continuous flow dynamic models*, Transportation Research Part B 100, 284–301, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trb.2017.02.002, identifies strict network-wide FIFO representation requirements including cohort bundles and distinctions from weaker FIFO; H. Bar-Gera and M. Carey (2022), *Constructing a cell transmission model solution adhering fully to first-in-first-out conditions*, Transportation Research Part B 161, 247–267, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trb.2022.04.002, constructs a high-complexity benchmark. These do not claim a Rust memory optimizer nor authorize eliminating physical source histories. Our specific lifetime equivalence is a **direct source-derived lemma**, not literature attribution.

Remaining scoped implementation risks: dropping an `incoming[i]` Part page before `combine_chunked_parts` is complete is not allowed; dropping `marks[i]` before computing the original ordered weighted-speed/byclass f64 reductions is not allowed; discounting source rows from `work.incoming` while keeping their Mark pages allocated is unsound. Correct fix couples **both** drops, then decrements only actual discharged `raw_rows`; the retained packet outputs and their `interval_retained` charges remain as before. Preserve unchanged B=128 and compact EventRow/Part codec, original numeric budget; no arbitrary 1.8KB waiver.
