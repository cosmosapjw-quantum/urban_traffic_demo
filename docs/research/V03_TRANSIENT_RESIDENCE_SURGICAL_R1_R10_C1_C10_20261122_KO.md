## R2/C2: original literature and past research mechanism, actual code mapping

Its cumulative arrival/departure process and occupancy conservation provide context, not our specific route/birth first-moment formula.
- [Bar-Gera & Carey, *Representation requirements for perfect FIFO verification* (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trb.2017.02.002) establishes why ordered cohort labels and original source order matter. ## R3/C3: transient age-stock conservation and faithful lossless-intern policy

For a fixed route label r across a traffic network with conserved internal transfers, define global in-network route stock \(N_r(t)\) (vehicle-equivalent mass), first origin-birth-time moment \(H_r(t)\) (mass seconds), and age-stock \(A_r(t):=tN_r(t)-H_r(t)\) (mass seconds). At external entry/exit, let \(dI_r,dO_r\) denote nonnegative mass measures and \(dB^{in}_r,dB^{out}_r\) the same carriers' signed first origin-birth-time moments; internal transfers preserve r and H and cancel globally. Source conservation:
\[
 dN_r=dI_r-dO_r,\qquad dH_r=dB^{in}_r-dB^{out}_r,\qquad
 dA_r=N_r\,dt+(t\,dI_r-dB^{in}_r)-(t\,dO_r-dB^{out}_r).
\]
Finite-horizon integrated identity, including jumps and nonstationarity, \(t\in[t_0,T]\):
\[
 \boxed{D^{out}_r
 =\int_{t_0}^{T}N_r(t)\,dt+
 D^{in}_r+A_r(t_0)-A_r(T)}
\]
where \(D^{in/out}_r:=\int(t\,dI_r-dB^{in}_r)\) or \(\int(t\,dO_r-dB^{out}_r)\). It is an EXACT pathwise conservation identity for finite nonnegative measures with finite first birth moments and bounded horizon, not a stationary \(L=\lambda W\) approximation. The input age term vanishes for vehicles genuinely newly born at entry, but not for previously aged vehicles entering the selected network. FIFO is NOT needed to prove this conservation identity; it IS needed to map each output moment to the original ordered source cohort history without ambiguity. Each internal edge (cell-to-cell transfer) contributes + and − the same mass and birth moment and therefore cancels without ordered-key global materialization.

Dimension check: \(N:\mathrm{veh}\), \(H,A,D:\mathrm{veh}\cdot s\), \(\int N\,dt:\mathrm{veh}\cdot s\). Three exact finite examples: (a) mass2 at t0 born0, mass1 exits at t3, mass1 remains at t5: occupancy area8, terminal age5, measured exit delay3. (b) mass1 enters at t2 already born at t=-1 (carried age3), leaves at t4: area2, entry age3, output age5. (c) initial mass1 at t0 born t=-2 exits at t1: area1, initial age2, output age3. They demonstrate that ignoring either endpoint or carried-age term corrupts transient delay.

Computational physics oracle contract: ordered finite boundary events (t,route,side,mass,birth_moment) with exact fractions.Fraction, strict conservation and nonnegative route stock at each event, initial stock and initial birth moment, piecewise-constant occupancy between boundary-event times. Compute sum interval*N_r, A(t0), A(T), D_in, D_out and assert equality route by route. No source sorting that reorders same-clock physical events; simultaneous events must be aggregated at their common clock before evaluating post-event stock.

Surgical C3 contract: RoadState::intern_exact_keysets() is a pure REPRESENTATION transformation on a completed candidate; physical Cell.v, totals, ledger, ordered packet target and iterated tuple (tag,offset,mass.to_bits,birth.to_bits,a_bits,b_bits) must remain identical. Admission postroad_continuous_keyset_peak_bytes remains conservative when selecting interning. If it exceeds the original limit, **do not allocate any transient group keyset**, return a valid uninterned candidate whose source packet rows already exist and whose post-road materialization guard has passed. Label keyset.surgical_deferred in trace. All full physical constraints and future native work budgets still enforce original limits; no fixed-byte haircut, no 8GiB threshold change. This is a reversible source-level revision to compulsory hot-path optimization, NOT authorization to change packet contents.
