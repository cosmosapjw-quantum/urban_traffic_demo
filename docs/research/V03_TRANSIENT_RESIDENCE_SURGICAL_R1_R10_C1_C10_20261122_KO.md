## R2/C2: original literature and past research mechanism, actual code mapping

- [Honnappa & Jain, *Transitory Queueing Networks* (2017), arXiv:1708.05921](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.05921) treats finite population, time-inhomogeneous arrivals, FIFO queues and time-dependent service. Its cumulative arrival/departure process and occupancy conservation provide context, not our specific route/birth first-moment formula.
- [Bar-Gera & Carey, *Representation requirements for perfect FIFO verification* (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trb.2017.02.002) establishes why ordered cohort labels and original source order matter. [Long et al. *Discretised link travel time models based on cumulative flows* (2011)](https://scispace.com/papers/discretised-link-travel-time-models-based-on-cumulative-4savh8o6vo?utm_source=chatgpt&utm_medium=tool) addresses FIFO cumulative inflow/outflow and interpolated link travel times. [Fralix & Riaño, *A new look at transient versions of Little's law* (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0021900200006756) and earlier transient Little-law queue literature show transient occupancy/delay relationships have precedent. **Global novelty of Little-type statements is NOT claimed**; the present explicit route-resolved birth-moment balance is a constructive in-project upgrade.
- Old \`research/v03-persistent-keyset-20261121\` and \`research/v03-keyset-continuous-20261121\` already implemented source \`RoadState::intern_exact_keysets()\`: exact elementwise ordered \`(tag,offset)\` match after hash hint, distinct immutable \`mass,birth\` moments, no altered numeric cells. That operation is invoked by checkpoint \`read_state\` and by continuous post-road interning at \`v1_road.rs::materialize_event_after_road()\`. Old simulation resumed via disk reload may implicitly intern before first physical step, while uninterrupted simulation pays the enormous optional transient allocation once per native call.
- The user-requested radical reconsideration: R1 baseline \`continuous keyset allocation\` **is not a physics conservation constraint**. It is a cross-packet representation optimization inserted *after all road physics and all EventRow flattening*. At 100k Valley t25710, preflight assumes every **40,717,830** uniform candidate row simultaneously acquires a second \`CorePart\` and every **737,875** candidate packet incurs another 512B, even if most physical route-key lists are unique or already shared; the source actually only interns groups with >=2 distinct backings and length>=16, and the original representation without interning is lossless. The physically necessary task is to deliver the new candidate unchanged; optimize only with provably affordable memory, not fail its physical state when optional canonicalization cannot be funded.
- Compared concrete approaches: (A) source-only **budget-aware lossless interning**, performing eager canonicalization if and only if a conservative peak budget fits, otherwise using original \`PacketParts::Uniform\`/existing \`SharedKeys\` without modifying any Part (selected initial surgical scope); (B) fine-grained two-phase streaming keylist interner with page-capacity staging, if A leaves later physical ticks blocked; (C) disabled-in-all-steps regardless of budget, which could squander real repeated-keyset savings; (D) haircut of the preflight by 508MB, rejected as invented cap relief; (E) reduced 100k residents or altered timestep, forbidden. The stage C5 test must prove A's decline is represented by an explicit \`DEFERRED_OPTIMIZATION\`, not \`V03 accepted\`, and must establish exact physical equality of source \`mass,birth,tag,offset,a,b\`, checkpoint wire and original per-road ledgers under both branches.
- The actual model's \`check_state\` and \`road\` residence aggregate can be cross-checked by a **route-resolved finite-time age-stock identity** for a labeled traffic network with internal transfers. Unlike a single mean queue delay, the identity includes a final residual-age term, source carried-age term, source/sink birth-moment flux and boundary initial stock; it is applicable to nonstationary, congested, partially completed routes. It has a constructive exact Fraction oracle and can later be tied to source-native global ledgers without materializing millions of cohort identifiers.
