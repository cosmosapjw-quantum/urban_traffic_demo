## S02: future eligibility sufficient statistic (derived)
For a fixed parent leg p, completion receipt measure rho_p=sum_i f_i delta_{t_i} with f_i>0. A fixed child c queries E_c(u)=int 1[t+d_c<=u] drho_p(t), using exact addition predicate in code; no past queries by release transition. Let D_p=max({d_c: parent(c)=p} union {0}). At committed clock T split A={i:t_i+D_p<=T}, R=complement. Replace A by M delta_bar_t, M=sum_A f_i, bar_t=sum_A f_i t_i/M, retain R unchanged in original order. Since bar_t is between min/max mature times, for any u>=T, d_c<=D_p, all A and representative satisfy the predicate. Thus all future child eligibility functions agree exactly in real arithmetic, total fraction and first completion moment are preserved, and first-moment-derived mean time since completion is preserved. This is an equivalence of these future observables under fixed child graph/dwells, not historical CDF, arbitrary new child, quantile, or bitwise floating-point equivalence. In binary64 use original addition test, clamp representative to extrema, fail closed if mature representative not eligible, total/moment conservation checked; retain immutable exact baseline. For no child D=0 all valid receipts at T are mature. For a bounded dwell D and max periodic sweep interval P (if swept regularly), retained history has at most one mature representative plus unmerged receipts with clocks > last_sweep-D; if arrival receipt rate per leg per tick bounded R, count <=1+R*ceil((D+P)/dt)+boundary rows, independent of entire previous horizon. R may be large; reduce separately by grouping new outlet flux.
