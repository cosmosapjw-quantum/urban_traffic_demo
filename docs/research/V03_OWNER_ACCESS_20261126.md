## S03 — finite access coupling and class-aware receiving theory

Let original origin/destination marginal delay laws be finite normalized measures eta_o=sum_i p_i delta_(a_i), eta_d=sum_j r_j delta_(b_j), with nonnegative delays in seconds. Partition [0,1] by the union of the two cumulative weight breakpoints. Each subinterval has length w_k>0 and constant quantiles (a_i,b_j). This constructs a common-rank coupling gamma=sum_k w_k delta_(a_i,b_j), with at most n_o+n_d-1 atoms. Proof: the subintervals within the i-th origin quantile interval exactly partition that interval, so sum_k w_k 1[a_i]=p_i; analogously for the destination. Total mass and both first delay moments are exact; for every departure measure mu, pushing mu tensor gamma forward under t+a_i gives exactly mu*eta_o (and under the unconstrained return clock plus b_j gives the other marginal). Rank pairing and causal dwell are retained in the event model; after congestion, return departures depend on realized outbound completion, so the joint access coupling matters. This is an explicit comonotone closure, not the independent product eta_o tensor eta_d and not native equivalence. It avoids up to n_o*n_d atoms and retains every original marginal atom, unlike mean access. A countermonotone or independent coupling is an admissible sensitivity arm.

For a class c with footprint ell_c in meters, define X_e,c (vehicles) and S_e=sum_c ell_c X_e,c (m); storage capacity K_e in meters. A feasible whole-head transfer of mass m requires m<=X_e,c, S_f+ell_c m<=K_f, and m<=R_f, where R_f is the remaining receiving budget in vehicles for the current 10 s tick (R_f=C_entry,f*h initially). Atomic update subtracts and adds m for the same class, and decrements R_f. Each internal move conserves class mass and summed physical standing length; source/sink ledger handles external changes. By induction S_e stays in [0,K_e] and R_f>=0. This provides a constructive class-coupled receiving condition preserving actual shared physical storage, not a separate road per class. Whole-head acceptance may leave unused residual supply; link-local FIFO is intentionally stronger than network partial FIFO. For split fluid service the maximal transferable head amount min(m,R_f,(K_f-S_f)/ell_c) has the same invariants but changes within-batch semantics; defer until independently verified.

Exact rational CDF and bounded integer storage checks are in access_law.py. The code models actual native class footprints 5,10,12 m and native entry/exit rates; the car projection remains a different dynamical closure.
