cardinality at least one; digest alone is excluded. No two-change minimum or
sufficient bundle is asserted.

- **Evidence:** E-001--E-005/E-016 support the scoped deficit, digest upper
  bound, overlap warning, and invariants, but no sufficient non-overlapping set.
- **Physical validity:** Exact geometry/topology/bytes/replay remain unchanged.
- **Mathematical validity:** One-run deficit arithmetic is sound; profiler-row
  addition and minimum-cardinality inference are not.
- **Novelty:** Ablation workflow, not a novel mechanism.
- **Testability:** V-001 has capacity, parity, wall, RSS, and kill criteria.
- **Robustness:** cache, environment, interaction, and instrumentation remain.
- **Tractability:** unchanged-byte exclusive-capacity measurement is cheapest.
- **Assumption burden:** an exact sufficient intervention is currently assumed.
- **Uncertainty:** capacity, interaction sign, stability, and transfer are open.

dissent would promote the measurement protocol, but that would blur problem
evidence with solution evidence. Measure perfect-removal capacity with paired
baseline/no-op controls, audit it, and return. No intervention is authorized.
